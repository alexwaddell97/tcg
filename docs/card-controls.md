# Arena card controls

- Click or tap a card in hand, on either side of the board, or in your queued plays to open its full card viewer. Board previews show current power, including arena and ongoing bonuses.
- Drag a hand card onto an arena to queue it. Legal arenas light up; the hovered arena and dragged card show whether the drop is valid. Lock in the turn to submit the plan.
- Drag a queued card to another arena to reposition it without changing reveal order or paying its cost again. Drag it back to the hand to remove it from the plan.
- Tapping a queued card inspects it. Use **Return to hand** in its viewer, or the board's **Undo** button, to undo a play.
- Release outside an arena to cancel. An invalid drop leaves the plan and energy unchanged. Full arenas can still receive spells.
- Cards remain inspectable while the turn is locked. Plays and edits are blocked during lock-in and reveal playback.

Mouse, pen and touch use the same pointer gesture. A small movement threshold distinguishes a tap from a drag. Pointer capture keeps the card attached during a drag; cancellation, loss of capture, a hidden page, a changed turn or a disconnected/locked match cancels it. The drag's final compatibility click cannot open the viewer accidentally.

For keyboard play, focus a hand card and press **Up Arrow** to pick it up, **Left/Right** to choose an arena, **Enter** to drop, or **Escape** to cancel. **Enter** on a card normally opens its viewer. Moving keyboard focus away cancels the pickup.
