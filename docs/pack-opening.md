# Interactive foil packs

Shop products and opening animations use dedicated generated foil artwork for each tier. Core uses `public/ui/packs/core-foil-v2.webp` (the original navy-and-gold design with its oversized baked-in branding removed); Expanded uses `expanded-foil-v2.webp` (sapphire foil, engraved silver constellation frame and aether compass), and Eternal uses `eternal-foil-v2.webp` (amethyst foil, gold eclipse frame and scrollwork). The built-in imagegen prompts and provenance are saved in `public/ui/packs/generation-v2.json`. The textures have their own materials and ornamentation; there are no hue or tint overlays. All three use the exact existing Arena Eternal logo composited at runtime with one shared size and position, so brand artwork stays consistent across the shop, preview and opening. Set names remain live text, centered in each wrapper's name plaque. Wrappers use a taller 3:5 silhouette across the shop, free-pack dock, preview and opening, while cards retain their 2:3 proportions.

Drag right across the top seal with a mouse, pen or touch. The wrapper tears progressively and keeps its progress on release or pointer cancellation. Pointer capture permits finishing outside the wrapper. At completion the strip flies off, the wrapper drops and the card emerges face down. It stays face down until clicked, tapped, or activated with Enter/Space. Legendary reveals retain their extra charge and particles after that input. Open pack is the keyboard alternative to tearing; Skip opening bypasses the wrapper but also leaves the card face down. Reduced-motion preferences bypass flying wrappers, particles and charging, while still requiring input to flip the card.

Opening a pack first plays a 2.4-second arrival: the sealed wrapper drops into a spotlight, lands with a light pulse and scattered sparks, settles, then catches a sweep of light across the foil. Only then do the tear controls appear. Core, Expanded and Eternal use their own pack colours. The sequence reveals no card details; the later flip is still manual. Skip opening remains available throughout, and reduced motion goes straight to the tear controls.

Purchasing and rewards are unchanged: the existing shop handler deducts currency and saves exactly one eligible card before the animation mounts. Gesture events never purchase or grant anything. Timers are cancelled on skip and unmount.

Use `/pack-preview` in development for Core, Expanded and Eternal previews and replay. It neither spends currency nor writes collection data.
