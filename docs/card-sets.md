# Card sets

The catalog uses three progression sets: Core Set, Expanded Set, and Eternal Set. Eternal is the home for recent headline cards and powerful build-around effects. Set membership is explicit metadata; assigning a set does not modify a card's power, cost, abilities, or rarity.

Current allocation:

- Core Set: 57 cards, including the entire balanced starter deck.
- Expanded Set: 72 cards spanning archetype tools and relics.
- Eternal Set: 11 cards, including the two season-pass cards scheduled to join its pack pool after their season ends.

`arenaSet` stores the progression set; `arenaRelease` preserves the named artwork/content release (Shattered Pacts or Relics). Future headline additions should be assigned to Eternal deliberately. No automatic demotion schedule is defined.

Collection and Deck Builder filter by set. Card inspection shows the set without adding text to card faces. The Shop offers free Core packs, 100-gem Expanded packs, and 150-gem Eternal packs, each restricted to its named set. Cards are acquired through packs or their premium season pass; crafting is removed. Current season-pass cards enter Eternal packs only after their season ends. Set rotation remains undecided. Variants remain cosmetic artwork of the same base card and share its set, mastery, and deck-copy limit.
